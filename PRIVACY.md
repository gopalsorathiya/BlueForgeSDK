# Privacy Policy

Novyble does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data Collection
The app does not collect any personal information such as name, email, or location.

## Bluetooth Usage
The app uses Bluetooth to scan and connect to nearby devices. This data is used only within the app and is not stored or transmitted externally.

## Data Sharing
No data is shared with third parties.

## Analytics
The app does not use analytics or tracking tools.

## Permissions
Bluetooth permission is required to discover and communicate with BLE devices.
